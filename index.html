01 · INTRODUCTION

What Is MahaDBT 2.0?

MahaDBT (Maharashtra Direct Benefit Transfer) Is The Government Of Maharashtra’s Online Platform For Scholarship, Education Fee Reimbursement, And Other Welfare Scheme Applications. Students Can Use It To Create An Account, Find Schemes, Apply Online, Upload Documents, Follow Application Verification, And Check Payment Progress.

Academic Year 2026–27: The Official MahaDBT Notice Says Fresh And Renewal Applications For 2026–27 Are Accepted Through MahaDBT 2.0. Confirm The Current Scheme List, Opening Dates, And Deadlines On The Portal.

02 · WHO SHOULD APPLY?

Check Your Eligibility

FYBA Students Studying In Maharashtra May Qualify For Different Schemes. Eligibility Depends On The Selected Scheme And May Consider:

  • Caste Or Beneficiary Category And Religion
  • Annual Family Income And Maharashtra Domicile
  • College, Course, Admission Type, And Academic Year
  • Previous Academic Results Or Merit Requirements
  • Disability, Hostel, Or Other Scheme-Specific Conditions

Categories May Include Open, EBC, EWS, SC, ST, OBC, VJNT, SBC, SEBC, Notified Minority Communities, And Students With Disabilities. Benefits And Conditions Differ. A Suggested Scheme Is Not A Guarantee Of Approval; The Department And Institute Make The Final Decision Under The Official Rules.

03 · PREPARE YOUR DOCUMENTS

Document Checklist

Personal And Contact

  • Aadhaar Card Or Other Identity Details Requested
  • Passport-Size Photograph And Signature
  • Active Mobile Number And Email Address

Education And Admission

  • SSC And HSC Marksheets
  • Current FYBA Admission Receipt
  • College Bonafide And Fee Receipt, If Requested
  • Previous-Year Marksheet For Renewal, Where Applicable

Category And Income

  • Income And Maharashtra Domicile Certificates, Where Required
  • Caste And Caste Validity Certificates, Where Applicable
  • Non-Creamy Layer Or EWS Certificate, If The Scheme Requires It
  • Disability Certificate, If Applying Under A Relevant Scheme

Bank And Hostel

  • Bank Passbook, Account Number, And IFSC
  • Check Aadhaar Seeding And NPCI Mapping When Required For DBT
  • Hostel Admission, Hostel Fee, Or Rent Proof For Relevant Benefits

These Are Examples, Not A Universal List. Each Scheme Has Its Own Document Checklist. Upload Clear, Readable Files In The Formats And Size Limits Shown By The Portal. Do Not Upload Password-Protected Files Unless The Portal Explicitly Supports Them.

04 · CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT

Register And Create Your Maha ID

  1. Open The Official MahaDBT 2.0 Website.
  2. Select Register And Follow The Registration Flow Currently Displayed. The Portal May Direct You To Its Integrated Registration Service.
  3. Enter Your Details Carefully. Keep Your Aadhaar And An Aadhaar-Linked Mobile Number Ready If Aadhaar OTP Verification Is Requested.
  4. Complete The Required OTP Or Identity Verification And Review Your Details.
  5. Save Your Maha ID And Login Information Securely. Never Share An OTP Or Password.

For Returning Applicants, Use The Login Method Offered On The Current Portal. Follow Its On-Screen Instructions If It Offers Maha ID, Aadhaar, Or Another Login Option.

05 · COMPLETE YOUR PROFILE

Check Your Student Dashboard

Complete The Profile Sections Requested By The Portal Before Searching Or Applying. These May Cover Personal And Contact Details, Address And Domicile, Family And Income, Category, Current Course, Past Qualification, Bank Information, And Hostel Details. Enter Information As It Appears In Your Official Records And Correct Any Mismatch Before Submission.

06 · FIND A SCHOLARSHIP

Use The Eligible Scheme Search

  1. Open The Apply For Scheme Or Scheme Discovery Section.
  2. Choose Find Eligible Schemes, Know Your Benefits, Or The Equivalent Option Shown In The Current Portal.
  3. Choose The Applicant Or Beneficiary Type For A College Student.
  4. Enter The Requested Details, Such As Domicile, Religion, Category, Gender, Income, Disability Status, College, Course, Admission Type, And Academic Year.
  5. Review The Suggested Schemes. Open Each Description And Check Its Eligibility, Government Resolution (GR), Tentative Benefits, And Required Documents.

Some Search Fields And Labels May Change. Use The Fields Shown In The Current Portal. A Scheme Suggestion Is Only A Starting Point; Final Eligibility Is Determined During Official Verification.

07 · SCHEMES TO INVESTIGATE

Examples For FYBA Students

These Examples Come From Different Departments And May Have Different Eligibility Rules. Check Whether Each Scheme Is Listed For Your Course And Academic Year Before Applying.

Directorate Of Higher Education

  • Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti
  • Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna
  • Eklavya Scholarship
  • State Government Open Merit Scholarship

May Consider Domicile, Income, Merit, Course, Admission, Or Hostel Conditions Depending On The Scheme.

Social Justice Department

  • Government Of India Post-Matric Scholarship
  • Post-Matric Tuition Fee And Examination Fee (Freeship)

Check The Current Category Rules And Eligible Fee Or Maintenance Benefits.

Tribal Development Department

  • Post-Matric Scholarship For ST Students

Eligibility And Benefits Follow The Applicable ST Scheme Guidelines.

OBC, VJNT And SBC Welfare

  • Post-Matric Scholarship To OBC Students
  • Post-Matric Scholarship To VJNT Students
  • Post-Matric Scholarship To SBC Students

Review Category, Income, Course, And Certificate Conditions On The Scheme Page.

Minority Development Department

  • State Minority Scholarship Part II (DHE)

Check The Current Notified Community, Course, And Benefit Requirements.

08 · SELECT YOUR SCHEME

Read The Rules Before You Apply

  1. Open The Scheme Description And Read The Eligibility Criteria.
  2. Review Its Government Resolution, Tentative Benefits, And Required Documents.
  3. Check That Your Course, College, Category, And Admission Match The Scheme Rules.
  4. Select Academic Year 2026–27 If It Is Available For That Scheme.
  5. Start The Application Only After You Have Confirmed The Scheme Is Appropriate For You.
09 · COMPLETE THE APPLICATION

Enter Your Information Accurately

Personal, Address, And Family

Provide Your Name, Date Of Birth, Contact, Address, Domicile, Parent Or Guardian, And Income Details As Requested. Make Sure They Match Your Supporting Records.

Course And Qualification

Use Your Actual Admission Details. For FYBA, This May Include B.A., First Year, Academic Year 2026–27, HSC As The Previous Qualification, Board, Seat Number, Marks, Admission Date, And Admission Type.

College And University

Enter The College And University Exactly As Shown In Your Admission Records. Nowrosjee Wadia College And Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) Apply Only If They Match Your Actual Admission.

Bank Details

Check The Account Holder Name, Bank, Branch, Account Number, IFSC, And Any Aadhaar Seeding Information Requested. Do Not Guess At Missing Details.

10 · UPLOAD DOCUMENTS

Review Every File

Upload The Documents Requested For Your Selected Scheme. A Common Checklist May Include:

DocumentWhat It May Support
Aadhaar Or Identity RecordIdentity Verification
Income CertificateFamily Income Conditions
Caste Or Validity CertificateCategory Conditions, Where Applicable
Domicile CertificateResidency Conditions, Where Applicable
HSC MarksheetPrevious Qualification
Admission Receipt Or BonafideCurrent College And Course

Hostel CertificateHostel-Related Benefits, Where Applicable

Some Information May Be Retrieved Through Integrated Government Services Or DigiLocker. Check Automatically Filled Details Before You Continue. The Current Portal’s Upload Instructions Take Priority On File Type And Size.

11 · PREVIEW AND SUBMIT

Final Review Checklist

  • Name And Identity Details Match Your Records.
  • Category, Income, And Domicile Information Are Correct.
  • College, Course, Admission Type, And Academic Year Are Correct.
  • Bank Details Are Accurate.
  • All Required Files Are Readable And Attached.

Save A Draft If You Need To Finish Later. Use Preview Application To Review The Whole Form. When Everything Is Correct, Submit It And Save The Application Reference Number (ARN). Keep A Copy Of The Submitted Form And ARN For Follow-Up Or A Grievance.

12 · COLLEGE VERIFICATION

What Happens After Submission?

Your Application Goes Through The Verification Workflow Set For The Scheme. The College May Check Admission, Course, Academic Year, Fees, Documents, And Other Institutional Details. The Application May Be Forwarded, Sent Back For Corrections, Or Rejected Under Applicable Rules.

If Your Application Is Sent Back

  1. Sign In And Open The Application Dashboard.
  2. Read The Return Reason Carefully.
  3. Correct The Requested Information Or Replace The Missing File.
  4. Review Your Changes And Resubmit By The Portal’s Deadline.

Do Not Ignore A Request For Corrections. Contact Your College Scholarship Section If The Reason Is Unclear.

14 · SCHOLARSHIP PAYMENT

Understand The DBT Payment Stage

Application Approval And Payment Disbursement Are Separate Stages. If A Payment Is Pending, Check The Portal’s Payment Information And Confirm That Your Bank Account Is Active, The Details Are Correct, And Aadhaar Seeding Or NPCI Mapping Is In Place If Required For The Scheme. Also Check Bank Messages Or Your Passbook And Contact Your Bank Or The Relevant Department If Needed.

15 · COMMON PROBLEMS

Quick Troubleshooting

Aadhaar OTP Did Not Arrive

Confirm The Mobile Number Linked To Aadhaar, Check Network Coverage, Wait Briefly, And Retry. Use The Portal’s Official Help Route If Authentication Continues To Fail.

College Or Course Is Missing

Check Your Department, Institute, And Course Filters. If The Correct Entry Is Still Missing, Contact Your College Scholarship Section To Confirm Its Portal Listing.

Application Was Sent Back

Read The Return Reason, Correct Only The Requested Fields Or Files, Then Review And Resubmit Before The Applicable Deadline.

Application Approved But Payment Is Pending

Check The Payment Status, Bank Details, Account Activity, And Aadhaar Seeding Or NPCI Mapping If Required. Contact Your Bank Or The Concerned Department For The Next Step.

Document Upload Failed

Use A Supported File Type, Follow The Size Limit Shown On The Upload Screen, Upload A Clear Scan, And Remove A PDF Password If The Portal Does Not Accept Protected Files.

16 · DO’S AND DON’TS

Do

  • Apply Before The Official Deadline.
  • Provide Accurate Details And Genuine Documents.
  • Save Your ARN And Check Status Regularly.
  • Respond To Correction Requests.
  • Confirm Your Bank Details.

Don’t

  • Enter Guessed Or Incorrect Income Information.
  • Upload Altered Certificates.
  • Share Your OTP Or Password.
  • Ignore A Sent-Back Application.
  • Assume Approval Means Immediate Payment.
  • Rely On Unofficial Agents.

18 · FINAL ADVICE

Start Early And Follow Through

Check Your Eligibility, Choose A Suitable Scheme, Prepare The Required Documents, Complete The Form Carefully, And Follow Up With Your College For Verification. Track The Application Until Its Final Status. Do Not Wait Until The Last Day.
